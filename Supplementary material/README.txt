Fig8: Contains the folders “Disprot” and “PDB” and Figure 8. It also contains the R codes “Disprot.R” and “PDB_missing.R”, used to generate the figure. 
The folder “Disprot” contains data on the disordered regions of proteins retrieved from the database DisProt, and the folder “PDB” contains data on the missing structural residues (from PDB) retrieved from UniProt.
 
Prerequisites:
R (version 4.3.3)
R Packages: ggplot2, tidyr, dplyr, readr
