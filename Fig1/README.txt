Fig1: Contains the relevant LCR metrics folders, Figure 1 and the R code (Fig1.R) used to generate the figure.

Subfolders:
Amino_acid: Amino acid counts of LCRs predicted across various methods
Diversity: Simpson’s Diversity Index and Shannon’s Entropy of LCRs predicted across various methods
LCR_Count: Number of LCRs predicted across various methods
LCR_Length: Length of LCRs predicted across various methods
LCR_Percov: Percentage coverage of LCRs predicted across various methods

Prerequisites:
R (version 4.3.3)
R Packages: ggplot2, viridis, reshape2, tidyverse, dplyr, tidyr
